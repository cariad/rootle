# rootle

> **rootle** /ˈruːt(ə)l/ *verb, informal, British*
>
> 1. To rummage about with cheerful purpose, poking into corners and lifting things up to see what's underneath. *"She rootled through the kitchen drawer for the good scissors."*
> 2. Of a pig: to turn over the earth with its snout, in hopeful search of something delicious. *"The pigs rootled happily under the oaks."*

Python functions to rootle around in your file system.
